Heisman Race

The Heisman Trophy is awarded every year to the best player in college football. The award was first given out in 1935, and Jay Berwanger was the first player to win it. In 1936, it was renamed the Heisman Trophy after John W. Heisman, a famous football coach. Since then, the award has become one of the most important awards in college football. Many great players have won it for their performances during the season. Today, the Heisman Trophy recognizes players who have had an outstanding season and made a big impact on their teams. The players listed below have stood out during the 2026 season because of their performances, statistics, and contributions to their teams.
